The grid includes all universities and institutes covered under the revised Tertiary Support Programme (TSP) policy, along with their respective disciplines and groups/programmes. In total, 180 universities/institutes have been included and mapped across the seven disciplines.

For clarity, the Health Sciences discipline has been split into two sections: Health Sciences (Main), which includes MBBS, BDS, and BSN, and Health Sciences (Other Programmes), which covers allied health, diagnostic, and related programmes.

For universities with multiple campuses, the main campus has been specified next to the university name, while the remaining campuses are listed at the end of the grid.

Please note that the policy and grid have now been finalized so if you are unable to find a particular programme or university in this document, it means that it was not included after multiple rounds of discussion or did not meet the TSP inclusion criteria.
