
WHAT IS BS IN RADIOLOGY?
A Bachelor of Science (BS) in Radiology is an undergraduate program that
trains students to become experts in medical imaging and radiologic
technology. The program typically spans 4 years (8 semesters) and covers
theoretical knowledge, practical skills, and clinical training in various imaging
modalities like X-rays, CT scans, MRIs, ultrasounds, and nuclear medicine.
Graduates are equipped to operate sophisticated imaging equipment, ensure
patient safety, and interpret diagnostic images to assist physicians in
diagnosing and treating diseases.

CORE AREAS OF STUDY
Medical Imaging Techniques: X-ray, CT, MRI, and ultrasound procedures.
Radiation Physics: Principles of radiation and its safe application in medical
settings.
Anatomy and Physiology: Understanding human body systems to interpret
imaging accurately.
Patient Care: Techniques to manage and ensure the comfort of patients
undergoing imaging procedures.
Radiation Safety: Knowledge of protocols to minimize exposure risks for patients
and professionals.
