Fashion & Textile - 4 years
What is it?
Fashion focuses on designing and producing clothing,
accessories, and styling trends, while textiles involve creating
fabrics, studying materials, and developing techniques like
weaving, knitting, and dyeing.

Some courses taught:
Fashion Design Basics: Sketching, patterns, and garment construction.
Textile Science: Fabrics, fibers, and finishes.
Fashion Illustration: Visual communication through sketches.
Digital Design Tools: Software like Adobe Illustrator, CorelDRAW, CLO 3D.
Pattern Making & Draping: Techniques for garment creation.
Fashion Marketing: Branding, sales, and e-commerce.
Sustainable Fashion: Eco-friendly and ethical practices.
Surface Embellishments: Embroidery, block printing, fabric manipulation.
Textile Technology: Advanced fabric production.
Apparel Production Management: Overseeing garment production lines.

Language & Literature - 4 Years
What is it?
A BA in Language & Literature is an undergraduate program
that studies languages, their structures, history, and literary
works across cultures and time periods. It emphasizes reading,
critical thinking, research, and writing skills, exploring different
genres (poetry, prose, drama) and literary theories to understand
how language reflects and shapes society.

Some courses taught:
Introduction to Literary Theory
World Literature
Creative Writing
Linguistics
History of English Literature
Comparative Literature
Cultural Studies
Advanced Composition and Rhetoric
Language Acquisition
Modern and Contemporary Literature
Classics and Mythology

You can work as a:
Publishing: Roles as editors, proofreaders, or literary agents.
Journalism: Writing for newspapers, magazines, or online
publications.
Teaching and Academia: Becoming an English teacher,
language instructor, or pursuing advanced studies (e.g.,
MA/PhD) to teach at the college or university level.
Content Writing and Copywriting: Creating content for
websites, advertising, marketing, or branding.
Public Relations: Managing communication strategies for
businesses and organizations.
Translation and Interpretation: Working as a translator or
interpreter, especially if the graduate is bilingual or
multilingual.
Corporate Communications: Writing and managing
corporate materials, reports, and internal communications.
Library Science: Working as a librarian or in archival and
research roles.
Creative Industries: Contributing to film, TV, or theater as a
scriptwriter, screenwriter, or literary consultant.
Nonprofit Sector: Writing grants, producing content, or
working in communications for NGOs.
Research and Analysis: Conducting literary or linguistic
research for academic institutions or think tanks.

Law & Criminal Studies - 4/5 Years
What is it?
A BA in Law & Criminal Studies is an undergraduate program that
provides an in-depth understanding of legal systems, crime, and
society's response to criminal behavior. It covers criminal law, legal
theory, justice systems, law enforcement, and rehabilitation,
blending theoretical knowledge with practical applications.
