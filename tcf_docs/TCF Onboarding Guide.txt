Welcome to University
You made it. Only 11% of Pakistanis make it to university each year - and you are one of them. This is not just an
academic milestone; it’s a turning point for you, your family, and your community.
TCF’s Alumni Pathways team is here to support you every step of the way. This guide summarizes everything covered
in your onboarding session.

About the TSP Scholarship
What is Qarz-e-Hasna?
Your scholarship is provided under the Qarz-e-Hasna model - a non-interest-bearing loan. TCF covers your university
fee with the expectation that you will repay it once you are financially able, so that future TCF students can receive
the same opportunity you have.
There is no fixed repayment deadline, but the sooner you repay, the more students can be helped.

Your Undertaking
By accepting the TSP scholarship, you have committed to the following:
   •   I will adhere to the TCF Tertiary Support Policy at all times.
   •   I will maintain at least 2.5 SGPA every semester.
   •   If my SGPA falls below 2.5 for two consecutive semesters, I understand my scholarship may be suspended.
   •   If I have any personal, academic, or social issue, I will reach out to the TCF team in a timely manner.
   •   If I am considering dropping out, I will speak to the TCF team before making any decision.
   •   If I drop out or am removed from my program, I will return the amount covered by TCF.
I will try to repay my scholarship so that future TCF students can benefit from the same opportunity
