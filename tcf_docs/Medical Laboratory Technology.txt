WHAT IS BS MLT/CLS?
A Bachelor of Science in Medical Laboratory Technology (also called
Clinical Laboratory Sciences) is a 4-year (8 semesters) undergraduate
program that trains students to perform diagnostic tests crucial for
disease detection, treatment, and prevention.
Graduates become skilled in laboratory testing, clinical research, and
managing lab operations to support physicians in accurate diagnosis and
patient care.

CORE AREAS OF STUDY
Clinical Pathology – Blood, urine, and body fluid analysis
Hematology – Blood disorders, complete blood counts, coagulation studies
Microbiology – Identifying infectious organisms, culture techniques, infection
control
Clinical Chemistry – Biochemical tests for liver, kidney, and metabolic disorders
Immunology & Immunohematology – Immune response, blood grouping,
transfusion medicine
Molecular Biology & Genetics – DNA/RNA-based diagnostics
Histopathology – Tissue preparation,
biopsy processing, cancer diagnostics
PRESENTED BY HANNAH MORALES
Laboratory Management & Quality Assurance – Lab safety, standards, and
reporting accuracy
Clinical Practicum – Hands-on training in hospital laboratories

COURSE OUTLINE
Year 1–2: Anatomy, Physiology, Microbiology, Biochemistry, Medical Terminology,
Basic Clinical Pathology
Year 3: Clinical Chemistry, Hematology, Histopathology, Immunology, Quality
Assurance
Year 4: Diagnostic Microbiology, Molecular Biology, Transfusion Medicine, Clinical
Diagnostics, Research & Lab Management
Practical Training: Clinical practicum in every semester from Year 2 onwards

WHY CHOOSE BS MLT/CLS?
✅ High Demand – Every hospital and diagnostic center requires skilled lab
technologists.
✅ Backbone of Healthcare – Over 70% of medical decisions rely on lab results.
✅ Career Flexibility – Opportunities in hospitals, research, pharmaceutical
companies, and diagnostic labs.
✅ Research & Innovation – Expanding role in molecular diagnostics and
personalized medicine.
✅ Job Growth – Increasing demand due to population growth and emerging
diseases.
