BIOTECHNOLOGY
4 YEARS, 8 SEMESTERS

A Bachelor's in Biotechnology typically spans 4 years and
focuses on using living organisms, systems, or biological
processes to create products or solutions that benefit society.
This field integrates molecular biology, genetics, microbiology,
biochemistry, bioinformatics, and chemical engineering.
Students learn about genetic engineering, bioprocessing,
bioinformatics, and the ethical implications of biotechnological
advancements.
What is BS in Biotechnology?

Emerging Field: With advancements in gene editing and synthetic
biology, biotechnology is a rapidly growing sector globally and in Pakistan.
Diverse Applications: Biotechnology has applications in health care
(vaccines, drugs), agriculture (GMOs, pest-resistant crops), industry
(biofuels, biodegradable plastics), and environmental conservation
(bioremediation).
Global and Local Demand: Biotechnology offers career opportunities in
academia, research, pharmaceuticals, and industrial sectors both locally
and internationally.
Problem-Solving: The field focuses on solving critical challenges like
disease treatment, food security, and environmental sustainability.
