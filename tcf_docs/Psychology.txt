emotional health while aligning with local cultural, social, and institutional
contexts.

understanding and addressing mental health challenges, educational
psychology, or behavioral science.

Career Opportunities in Pakistan
Clinical Settings:
Work as a psychologist in hospitals, rehabilitation centers, or private clinics.
Educational Institutions:
School psychologist or counselor to support students' mental health.
Corporate Sector:
Industrial-organizational psychologist for employee well-being and performance
enhancement.
NGOs and Community Work:
Work with NGOs addressing mental health awareness, trauma counseling,
or community support.
Academia and Research:
Teach psychology at colleges and universities.
Contribute to research on mental health issues in Pakistan.

Please Note:
A BS in Psychology alone typically does not qualify you to practice as a
licensed psychologist or clinical therapist. Most professional roles, especially
in clinical psychology, require advanced degrees (MS, MPhil, or even PhD).
For example, to become a clinical psychologist or counselor, you would need
to pursue MS Clinical Psychology or an MPhil in the relevant specialization.
This would provide you with the necessary practical training, internships,
and research experience required for licensure.
The Pakistan Psychological Association (PPA) and other regulatory bodies
often require a Master’s degree for professional practice.
