What is CS?
Computer Science (CS) is a 4 year degree that includes computing
systems, algorithms, software, and data processing.
It involves designing, developing, and maintaining software
applications, analyzing data, creating algorithms, and solving complex
computational problems using technology. The field also encompasses
artificial intelligence (AI), machine learning, data science,
cybersecurity.

Why Pursue Computer Science?

High Demand: With the rise of technology, there is an increasing demand
for computer science professionals in Pakistan, especially in software
development, IT services, cybersecurity, and data analysis.
Growing Tech Industry: Pakistan's IT industry is expanding, with a high
demand for skilled professionals in both local and international markets.
Global Opportunities: Computer science skills are recognized worldwide,
and Pakistan’s growing IT exports open doors for global employment
opportunities.
Innovation and Future Impact: With advancements in AI, blockchain, and
IoT (Internet of Things), computer scientists play an essential role in
driving technological change and innovation.
