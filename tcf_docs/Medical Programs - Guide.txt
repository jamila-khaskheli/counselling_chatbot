Bachelor of Science in Cardiac Perfusion (4 years)
What is Cardiac Perfusion?
It is a health-care job where you help the heart and
lungs work during surgery.
When a patient is having open-heart surgery, the heart
might need to stop temporarily. A perfusionist uses a
heart-lung machine to take over the work of the heart
and lungs. To make sure blood keeps flowing, oxygen
reaches all parts of the body, and the patient stays alive
and stable while the surgeon operates.

After graduation you can work as:
Cardiac Perfusionist / Perfusion Technician
Surgical / Operation Theatre Assistant
ICU / Critical Care Support Staff
Medical Equipment Technician
Advanced Specialization

Courses Taught
Intro to Perfusion Technology
Cardiovascular anatomy
cardiopulmonary bypass (CPB)
Blood‑gas & blood‑conservation studies
Perfusion chemistry
Ventricular‑assist devices

Bachelor of Science in
Anesthesia (4 years)
The curriculum is based on clinical training with hands‑on
operating theatre (OT) work, ICUs, pre‑/ post‑operative care,
assisting anesthesiologists and surgical teams. You learn to
assist anesthesiologists in surgeries, manage and monitor
anesthesia equipment, monitor patient vital signs under
anesthesia.
BS Anesthesia Technology trains you to be a technical /
paramedical professional in anesthesia delivery and
patient‑care support (not a doctor, but a vital part of surgical /
OT / ICU teams).

What is Bachelors of Science in Orthotics?
This degree teaches you to help people who have lost limbs
or have movement problems. You learn both medical
knowledge (how bones, muscles, and joints work) and
technical skills (how to make artificial limbs, braces, and
supports).
The goal is to help people walk, move, or function
normally after injury, illness, or disability.
