
Bachelors in
Business Administration (BBA)
It is an undergraduate degree that focuses on developing a comprehensive
understanding of business operations and management.
It prepares students to navigate the complexities of the corporate world by equipping
them with theoretical knowledge, practical skills, and a strategic mindset.
It spans for 4 years with 8 semesters.

Why you should pursue BBA
Builds a Strong Foundation in Business: A BBA helps you learn the basics of how
businesses work, from managing people to making financial decisions.
Opens Up Job Opportunities: You can work in many fields like marketing, finance,
human resources, and management.
Helps You Develop Important Skills: You’ll improve your communication, leadership,
and decision-making skills, which are valuable in any career. You’ll also learn to think
critically and solve problems.
Flexible Career Options: You can work in different industries like banking, technology,
marketing, or even government. It’s a degree that fits almost everywhere.
Encourages Entrepreneurship: If you dream of starting your own business, a BBA
teaches you how to plan, manage finances, and lead a team, giving you the tools to
succeed.
High Demand for Business Professionals: Companies need managers and business
experts, so there’s always demand for BBA graduates. This means you have good
chances of finding a job.

Career path beyond BBA
After completing a BBA, you can:
Enter the workforce directly in roles like marketing executive, financial
analyst, or HR officer.
Pursue further education, such as an MBA, MS in a specific area, or
certifications like CFA (Chartered Financial Analyst) or PMP (Project
Management Professional).
Explore entrepreneurship and start your own business.
