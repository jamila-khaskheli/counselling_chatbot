Degree Awareness
✔
What degree do you want to study and how long is it?
Example: 4-year BS, 5-year professional degree, etc.
✔
Which intermediate faculty is connected to your degree?
(Pre-Medical, Pre-Engineering, Commerce, Humanities, ICS, etc.)
✔
What subjects or courses will you study in this degree?
Be ready to name key subject areas related to your field.
Review the program curriculum from universities websites for this.

Degree Awareness
✔
Which universities in Pakistan offer your chosen degree?
Also think about: Which university do you prefer and why?
Its also important to know the location of the universities you want to apply to.
✔
What academic skills or strengths are helpful for this degree?
(Examples: Math Skills, Analytical Thinking, Communication, Empathy)
✔
What jobs or careers can you pursue after this degree?
Know at least 5 entry level job positions/roles.
✔
Which companies or organizations hire graduates in your field?
Know the names of a few relevant employers.
(Examples: HBL, UBL from the banking sector, hospitals and clinical labs like Ziauddin,
DOW or Essa Labs or software companies like Systems Lt., 10Pearls etc.)

Future Goals & Motivation
Interviewer may ask:
Why do you want to study this degree?
How did you choose this field?
What are your long-term goals?
Where do you see yourself after completing this degree?

English Suitability
You may be asked simple questions in English
such as:
“Can you tell me about your daily routine?”
“Why is English important for your degree?”
They are checking confidence — not perfection.

Family Support & Background
Expect questions like:
“Who helps you make education decisions at home?”
(Understanding how supportive your family is)
“Do you or your siblings work to support the family?”
(Checking responsibilities or financial pressure)
“Are your siblings currently studying?”
(To understand the education environment at home)

Tips for Success
Research your degree properly.
Know at least 3–4 universities offering your program.
Be clear and honest about your goals.
Speak slowly, clearly, and confidently.
Show motivation and readiness for university.

Best of luck!
