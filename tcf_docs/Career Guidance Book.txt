Career Guidance Book

HOW TO USE THIS GUIDE
This Career Guidance Book is your starting point; not your
finishing point. It is designed to give you a clear overview of the
fields available to you, the programs you can study, and the kinds
of careers they lead to. But the research does not stop here.
STEP 1 — Start here
Read through the section for each field that interests you. Look at the
programs, the road map, and the career paths. Ask yourself: does this
feel like something I could see myself doing?
STEP 2 — Go deeper with university websites
Once you have a shortlist of fields, visit the official websites of the
universities listed under 'Universities on Policy'. Read about their
specific programs, admission requirements, fee structures, and
campus life.
STEP 3 — Use Google and AI tools
Search for things like 'what does a [career name] do in Pakistan' or
'difference between CS and Software Engineering'. Use AI tools like
Claude or Google to ask follow-up questions and explore further.
These tools can help explain concepts in simple language.

problems. Engineers work across industries including infrastructure, energy,
manufacturing, technology, aviation, textiles, and chemicals.

FREE LEARNING RESOURCES
Below are free online resources organised by discipline. Use these alongside
your university research to build subject knowledge, explore career paths,
and strengthen your preparation.
I want to explore what different careers actually look like
Not sure what engineers, doctors, or designers actually do every day? Start here.
CrashCourse (YouTube) — Short, engaging videos on almost every field — Business,
Psychology, Engineering, Medicine, and more
TED-Ed (YouTube / ted.com) — Real professionals talk about their work and ideas in plain
language
LinkedIn Learning previews (LinkedIn app) — Search any job title and watch free preview
videos of what that career involves
I want to start learning the basics of my field
Once you have a field in mind, these platforms let you take real university-level courses
for free.
Khan Academy (khanacademy.org) — Covers Math, Science, Computing, Economics —
everything explained from scratch, at your own pace
Coursera (coursera.org) — Choose "Audit" on any course to access all videos and readings
for free. Covers every major field
edX (edx.org) — Free courses from MIT, Harvard, and other universities — audit mode is
always free
I want to improve my English
Strong English will help you in university, in interviews, and in your career. These make it
easy to practise daily.
BBC Learning English (bbc.co.uk/learningenglish) — Short daily lessons, news-based
listening practice, and grammar exercises
Duolingo (app) — A few minutes a day builds vocabulary and reading confidence over
time
EnglishClass101 (YouTube) — Clear lessons on grammar, vocabulary, and spoken English
for different levels
I want to prepare for entry tests and admissions
Entry tests like ECAT, MDCAT, and BCAT require consistent practice.
PakMCQs (pakmcqs.com) — Pakistan-specific MDCAT, ECAT, and NTS past papers and
practice questions
Khan Academy Math & Science (khanacademy.org) — Revise the topics that come up in
entry tests — Algebra, Physics, Chemistry, Biology
YouTube — search your test name (YouTube) — Search "MDCAT preparation 2025" or
"ECAT tips" to find Pakistani students and teachers sharing strategies
